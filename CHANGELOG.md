## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-28
- First version: an original top-down RC truck racer with 4 tracks, 3 AI rivals, 3 modes (Ridgeline Cup, Single Race, Time Trial) and 3 difficulty levels.
- Hazards and features: oil slicks, stray bouncing wheels, boost pads, ramps over mud pits, nitro pickups and wrong-way detection.
- Audio: procedural chiptune music (race and menu themes), sound effects, an engine and skid sound, and a text-to-speech announcer with on-screen captions.
- Scoreboard saved between sessions: top 5 races and laps per track with initials, cup results and career stats.
- Desktop apps: an Electron 44.7.1 wrapper with a universal macOS `.dmg` (ad-hoc signed) and a Windows x64 `.zip`. The apps include their fonts and run offline.
